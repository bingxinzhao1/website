Bingxin Zhao

I am an associate professor in Statistics and Data Science at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, with a secondary appointment in the Department of Medicine at the Perelman School of Medicine.

My research develops statistical and AI methods for science and medicine, with a focus on integrating multimodal data, such as imaging, genomics, and electronic health records, to enable discovery and translation. My work has advanced understanding of the brain and inter-organ connections (e.g., heart–brain links) in relation to brain disorders and healthy aging. I have also contributed to unifying statistical rigor, software engineering, and agentic AI to build practical systems for scientific and medical research.

Research

My work mainly spans across four directions:

  • Inter-organ connections: Heart-brain connections (Science 2023), Multi-organ imaging genetics (Nature Reviews Neurology 2026), Multi-organ Mendelian randomization (Nature Biomedical Engineering 2026), Rare coding variants across organs (Nature Communications 2026), Plasma proteomics and organ imaging (Nature Communications 2026), Eye-brain connections (Nature Communications 2024)

Platforms for the research community

We build publicly accessible cloud platforms that make advanced statistical and genetic analyses available to researchers worldwide, without the need for local computing resources.

PennPRS
Train polygenic risk score models online from GWAS summary statistics, with no individual-level data or local computing needed. Supports single- and multi-ancestry training with multiple methods, direct access to GWAS Catalog data, and free pretrained weights for thousands of phenotypes.
Preprint (Nature Methods, accepted)
pennprs.org
BIGA
Analyze the genetics shared across traits and diseases from GWAS summary statistics in the cloud. Offers unified pipelines such as genetic correlation, curated GWAS data for thousands of phenotypes, and tools to upload data, submit jobs, and share results.
Paper (Journal of the American Statistical Association 2026)
bigagwas.org
TigerAI
Query AI-generated genetic evidence on drug targets to judge which target–indication pairs are likely to succeed in clinical development. Its evidence is as informative as expert curation with far broader coverage, and the platform also benchmarks the latest large language models.
Preprint (bioRxiv 2026)
tigerai.bio
PASS
An agentic AI system that evaluates a manuscript and positions it within its field: it finds the closest related work, traces how the topic has developed, and predicts which journals it fits best. Works from the abstract alone and outperforms general-purpose LLMs and established journal-finder tools.
Preprint (arXiv 2026)
ratemypaper.ai
